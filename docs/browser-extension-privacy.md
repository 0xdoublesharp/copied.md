# copied.md browser extension privacy

copied.md converts Markdown on your device. It reads selected text or text you enter when you invoke a copy or conversion action. When you choose a destination format, copied.md writes the converted text to your clipboard. It does not send the Markdown, clipboard contents, or page content to a server.

You can separately enable copied.md on ChatGPT and Claude in Settings. On an enabled site, copied.md checks the page for assistant replies and adds a **Copy as…** button. It reads the reply you choose to convert. You can turn off access to either site in Settings.

The extension stores your settings, enabled chat sites, and most recently used copy format in the browser’s local extension storage. It does not use tracking or analytics.

copied.md’s use of information received from Google Chrome APIs adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies), including its Limited Use requirements.

For a privacy question, contact [the copied.md project](https://github.com/0xdoublesharp/copied.md/issues).
